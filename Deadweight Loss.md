# Deadweight Loss

$$\text{Deadweight Loss} = \frac{1}{2} \times T \times (Q^* - Q_T) $$


Where T = tax
Q* = Quantity Sold Before tax
Qt = Quantity Sold After tax

When the tax Rates increases 2x 
Deadweight loss increases 4x

So as Tax Rates Increases Deadweight Loss increases very much 

When Deadweight Loss is greater than [[Market efficiency]] The trade doesn't occur
