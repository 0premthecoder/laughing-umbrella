# Competitive Management Frame

|**Business Domain**|**Frame A**|**Frame B**|**Strategic Consequence**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Customer Exchange**|Transactional exchange|Ongoing relationship|Shifts focus from one-off sales to Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).|
|**Customer Complaints**|Legal liability / Threat|Free informant / Early warning signal|Shifts posture from risk defense to process improvement.|
|**Negotiation**|Win-Lose zero-sum game|Win-Win joint problem solving|Shifts outcome from value extraction to value creation.|
|**Competitors**|Entities stealing customers|Entities stealing profits|Shapes defensive, pricing, and positioning tactics.|
|**Time**|Finite input to productivity|Background setting of life/work|Alters timeboxing, prioritization, and scheduling strategies.|
|**Employee Training**|Operational expense|Long-term asset investment|Determines budget priority during economic downcycles.|
|**Work Groups**|Interchangeable parts of a machine|Sports teams / Communities of practice|Impacts morale, collaboration, and delegation dynamics.|
|**Organization**|Nexus of legal contracts|Community with shared purpose|Shapes leadership style, alignment, and corporate culture.|
