# Groupthink

- **Core Mechanism:** A desire for group harmony, loyalty, or consensus suppressing critical evaluation and honest dissent.
    
- **Key Symptoms:**
    
    - **Illusion of Unanimity:** Interpreting silence as complete agreement.
        
    - **Self-Censorship:** Members suppressing personal doubts to avoid being labeled as non-team players.
        
    - **Illusion of Invulnerability:** Assuming past success guarantees future invincibility.
        
    - **Stereotyping Outsiders:** Dismissing external perspectives (_"Consultants don't understand our business"_).
        
- **Remedies:** Designed dissent, appointing a devil’s advocate, anonymous polling prior to discussions, and having junior team members speak first.
