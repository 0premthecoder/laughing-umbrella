# Thinking Frame

A mental lens, filter, or frame of reference through which individuals interpret reality, define problems, and evaluate solutions.

- **Core Function:** A frame systematically addresses three foundational questions:
    
    1. _What is happening?_
        
    2. _Why is it happening?_
        
    3. _What should we do about it?_

Managers are often oblivious to the mental frames through which they view reality. Frames filter what we notice and are naturally sticky, exclusive, and difficult to perceive from within.

### Key Properties of Frames

- **Selective Filtering:** They define what details are captured and what details are excluded from awareness (like a picture frame placed in front of a vast landscape).
    
- **Self-Contained & Complete:** Inside a specific frame, the logic appears entirely whole and self-consistent.
    
- **Domain & Functional Bias:** Training, career background, and organizational role shape an individual's default framing.
