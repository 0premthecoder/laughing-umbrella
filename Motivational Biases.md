# Motivational Biases

- **Source:** Personal drive, self-esteem, career focus, status, or compensation incentives.
    
- **Risk:** Distorted incentives, lack of personal accountability, and resource waste.
    
- **Key Types:**
    
    - **Self-Serving Bias:** Attributing success to internal ability while blaming failure on external market factors.
        
    - **Escalation of Commitment:** Continuing to fund a failing initiative (sunk cost fallacy) to protect reputation or avoid admitting a prior bad decision.
        
    - **Optimism Bias:** Believing favorable outcomes are far more likely than base rates justify (e.g., assuming a product launch will take 6 months when reality dictates 18).
        
    - **Status Quo Bias:** Preferring current conditions and avoiding structural change out of fear of disruption (e.g., retaining outdated ERP software).
        
    - **Incentive-Induced Bias:** Judgment distorted by reward structures (e.g., investment bankers downplaying deal risks to secure transaction fees).
