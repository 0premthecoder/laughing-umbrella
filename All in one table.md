# All in one table

| **Fallacy**             | **Core Mechanism**                       | **Business Example**                                   | **Key Impact**                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Ad Hominem**          | Attacks individual's traits              | Dismissing valid feedback due to age or department     | Ignores valid insight           |
| **Bandwagon**           | Relies on popular adoption               | Investing in tech solely because peers do              | Blind herd mentality            |
| **False Cause**         | Confuses sequence/correlation with cause | Attributing sales growth entirely to a logo change     | Misallocated credit/capital     |
| **Straw Person**        | Exaggerates argument to attack it        | Framing partial task automation as job destruction     | Halts operational progress      |
| **False Dilemma**       | Forces an either/or choice               | _"Cut 20% staff or company fails"_                     | Obscures middle-ground options  |
| **Slippery Slope**      | Assumes an extreme chain reaction        | _"1 day remote work will destroy office culture"_      | Prevents reasonable flexibility |
| **Circular Reasoning**  | Premise equals conclusion                | _"It's innovative because it's an innovative culture"_ | Zero analytical depth           |
| **Appeal to Tradition** | Past usage equals present validity       | Keeping 30-year-old processes despite tech shifts      | Causes organizational inertia   |
| **Appeal to Novelty**   | New equals better                        | Buying new AI tools expecting magic improvements       | Unverified capital spending     |
| **Appeal to Emotion**   | Replaces data with feelings              | Continuing projects because _"people worked hard"_     | Protects non-performing assets  |
| **Sunk Cost**           | Past expense justifies future spending   | Continuing Concorde-like projects due to past spend    | Escalates financial loss        |
| **Red Herring**         | Deflects to irrelevant topics            | Answering profit drops with satisfaction scores        | Dodges accountability           |
| **Survivorship Bias**   | Evaluates only successful survivors      | Copying college dropouts to build unicorns             | Skews risk management           |
| **Loaded Question**     | Embeds built-in accusation               | _"Why does marketing keep wasting money?"_             | Prematurely places blame        |
| **Nirvana Fallacy**     | Rejects imperfect progress               | Rejecting safety upgrades because risks remain         | Preserves me                    |
