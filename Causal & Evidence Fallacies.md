# Causal & Evidence Fallacies

- **False Cause (_Post Hoc_):** Assuming that because Event B followed Event A, Event A caused Event B (e.g., claiming a logo redesign caused a sales spike when market conditions shifted).
    
- **Survivorship Bias:** Drawing conclusions purely from successful outcomes while ignoring hidden failure cases (e.g., claiming college dropouts make better founders by ignoring thousands who failed).
    
- **Circular Reasoning:** Assuming the conclusion within the initial premise without independent evidence (e.g., _"This company is innovative because it has an innovative culture"_).
