# Monopoly vs Perfect Competition

### Market Structure: Monopoly vs. Competitive Markets

- **Price Determination:**
    
    - **Competitive Firm:** A **price taker**. Price is determined by market demand and supply ($P = MC$). The firm accepts the market price and chooses the quantity to produce that maximizes profit.
        
    - **Monopoly Firm:** A **price maker**. The firm sets the price above marginal cost ($P > MC$), creating a markup over cost.
        
- **Absence of a Supply Curve in Monopoly:**
    
    - Monopolists **do not have a supply curve**.
        
    - A supply curve shows the quantity a firm chooses to supply at any given price (taking price as given). Because a monopolist simultaneously chooses price and quantity based on marginal cost and demand, there is no unique relationship between price and quantity supplied.
        
    - Standard supply and demand theory applies strictly to **perfectly competitive markets**.
