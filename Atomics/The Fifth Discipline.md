# The Fifth Discipline

In his seminal work _The Fifth Discipline_, Peter Senge argues that modern organizations fail not because of external forces, but because of systemic **learning disabilities**. 
These disabilities are predictable consequences of structural designs and entrenched mental models that prioritize short-term firefighting over long-term capability building.

To overcome these disabilities, organizations must cultivate **five core disciplines**, with **Systems Thinking** serving as the integrating "Fifth Discipline."
