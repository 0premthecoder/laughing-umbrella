# Nine Components of Integrative Management

- **Purpose & Value:** What problem matters and for whom; legitimacy sought.
    
- **Strategic Choices:** Where to play, how to win, what deliberately not to do.
    
- **Business Model & Ecosystem:** 9 components (partners, complements, channels, value capture, networks).
    
- **Capabilities & Structure:** Critical capabilities, decision rights, coordination, ambidexterity.
    
- **Processes & Flows:** End-to-end customer value streams, interfaces, bottlenecks, feedback loops.
    
- **People & Culture:** Skills, incentives, leadership, identity, psychological safety, learning.
    
- **Data, Tech & AI:** Architecture, data quality, automation, augmentation, cyber resilience.
    
- **Governance & Adaptation:** Risk, ethics, controls, metrics, experiments, reviews, renewal.
    
- **Time Horizons:** _Now_ (performance), _Next_ (scaling), _Beyond_ (options & transformation).
