# Shifting the Burden

- **Mechanism:** A problem is addressed via an easier symptomatic solution rather than a fundamental solution, creating side effects and dependencies that atrophy the capability for long-term resolution.
    
- **Example:** Boeing re-engineered the 737 airframe and relied on the MCAS software patch instead of designing a new aircraft platform, leading to fatal crashes.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Make side effects explicit and measurable; protect investment in fundamental solution
