# Hegelian Inquiring System

IG.W.F. Hegel (1770-1831) introduced the dialectical method - the idea that thought and
history advance not through linear accumulation but through the productive collision of
opposites.

In Churchman's framework, the Hegelian Inquiring System confronts ill-structured problems -
problems where objectives conflict,

- Truth is conflictual.
- Knowledge advances through the productive collision of a Thesis and its Antithesis into a creative Synthesis.
- Best suited for ill-structured problems, conflicting objectives, and situations requiring intuitive or synthetic reasoning.
