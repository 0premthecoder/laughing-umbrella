# Purva Mimamsa

Founder: Jaimini: Mimamsa Sutra (3rd Century BCE)
Astika: Ritualist, Pluralist

### Core Doctrine

Vedic Injuctive Statements (Vidhi) are Valid Commands for Dhrama (Right Action)
- Which is known through correct explaination

RIght action leads to potency (apurva) which yields greater results

Vedas (Testimony) are primary pramana(validation) for Study of Matters of the duty

Western Counterpart: Kantian Deontology , western hermeneutic tradition
