# Global & National AI Governance Frameworks

- **EU AI Act (2024):** Risk-tiered classification (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal) with strict conformity assessments for high-risk domains.
    
- **India NITI Aayog Responsible AI for All (2021):** Guidelines covering safety, equality, privacy, and domain-specific adoption.
    
- **India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023):** Mandates data principal rights, including explanation rights for automated decisions.
    
- **OECD AI Principles (2019):** 5 principles (inclusive growth, human-centered values, transparency, robustness, accountability) adopted across 46 nations.
    
- **IEEE Ethically Aligned Design:** Technical ethics and human-rights alignment standards.
    
- **ISO 42001 (2023):** International standard for enterprise AI Management Systems.
