# Types of System

- **Closed Systems:** Theoretically isolated with no exchange of matter, energy, or information with the external environment (e.g., a sealed gas container). Used in business strictly as analytical simplifications.
    
- **Open Systems:** Actively exchange resources (capital, talent, raw materials) and information with the external environment. **All business organizations are open systems.**
