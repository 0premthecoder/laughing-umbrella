# Growth and Underinvestment

- **Mechanism:** Growth hits capacity limits, but managers fail to invest in necessary capacity expansion. Performance degrades, demand drops, and the drop in demand is used post-hoc to justify the lack of investment.
    
- **Example:** Sears focused on financial engineering, buybacks, and real estate monetization instead of investing in store experience and supply chain modernization, losing out to Walmart and Amazon.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Make capacity investments prospectively based on future demand rather than current degraded performance.
