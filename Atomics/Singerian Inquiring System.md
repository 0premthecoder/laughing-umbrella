# Singerian Inquiring System

Edgar Arthur Singer Jr. (1873-1954), a studer
of William James and one of the architects of American pragmatism.

Singer argued that knowledge is always in the service of purpose, and that behind every
scientific or technical system lie ethical presuppositions.
