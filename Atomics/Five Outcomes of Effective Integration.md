# Five Outcomes of Effective Integration

- **Coherence:** Priorities, resources, and operating metrics reinforce one another (indicator: lower conflict, faster execution).
    
- **Adaptability:** Senses change, experiments, and reconfigures capabilities (indicator: time from signal to tested response).
    
- **Resilience:** Absorbs shocks without losing critical purpose (indicators: recovery time, flow redundancy).
    
- **Legitimacy:** Incorporates ethics, stakeholder interests, and regulations (indicators: trust, compliance, social license).
    
- **Learning:** Decisions generate evidence for future judgment (indicator: rate of completed learning cycles).
