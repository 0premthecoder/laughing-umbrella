# Free trade

Trade can make everyone better off 

Inward-Oriented Policies 
- Aim to Raise Living Standards by avoiding interaction with other Countries
- e.g Tariffs, limits on investment from abroad

Outward Oriented policies
- Promote Integration with world economy
- e.g Elimination on restiriction of trade

Trade Occurs more freely in Outward Oriented policies which fosters growth
