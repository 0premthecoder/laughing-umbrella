# Various Forms Of Money - Measures of Money Supply

M1 = Currency notes and coins you carry in your wallet + money in your current/savings
bank account that you can withdraw anytime.

M2 = M1+Saving deposits of Post Office Savings Banks(Small-denomination time
deposits)

M3 = M2+ Fixed Deposits /Time deposits with the banking System (large-
denomination time deposits money locked in for a period)

M4 = M3 +All deposits with the post office savings bank
