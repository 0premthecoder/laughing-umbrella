# Factors of Productivity

Physical capital per worker (K/L)
- The stock of equipment and structures used to produce goods and services.
- Leads to better incentive to work for workers
- An increase in K/L raises Y/L - but with diminishing returns

Human capital per worker (H/L)
- The knowledge and skills workers acquire through education, training, and experience.
- educated worker can use capital more effectively

Natural resources per worker (N/L)
- Inputs from nature: land, rivers, mineral deposits.
- Could be Renewable or Non-renewable
- Human capital and technology matter far more than resource endowments.

Technological knowledge (A)
- Society's understanding of the best ways to produce goods and services.
- Advances in technology raise productivity for all workers who adopts them.
