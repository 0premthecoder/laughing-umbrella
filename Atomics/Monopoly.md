# Monopoly

A [[What is a Firm|Firm]] is a Monopoly if
- it is the only seller of its Product
- its product doesn't have close substitutes 

### Reasons Led to monopoly

The existence of Entry Barriers is fundamental cause 
- Sources of Entry Barriers
	- Ownership of Key resource by a Firm
	- Got Exclusive Right to sold by Govt (EIC) {Patents could also give chance for Monopoly}
	- Firms are efficient into producing while others aren't
		- [[Natural Monopolies]]
