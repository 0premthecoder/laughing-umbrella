# Practical Advice for Stock and Flow

- **Name variables as nouns or noun phrases that can increase or decrease:** Use "customer satisfaction," not "satisfy customers."
    
- **Avoid vague variables:** Use "average delivery lead time" rather than "operations problem."
    
- **Assign link polarity carefully:** Ask: if variable A increases, what happens to variable B compared with what it otherwise would have been?
    
- **Mark important delays:** Most managerial surprises arise because corrective action arrives late.
    
- **Look for closed loops:** A chain of causes is not yet systems thinking until it returns to influence itself.
    
- **Separate stocks from flows:** Ask whether the variable accumulates over time. If yes, it is likely to be a stock.
    
- **Avoid over-drawing:** A useful diagram is a disciplined argument, not a map of everything.
