# Vaisheshika School

Founder: Kanada - Vaisheshika Sutra (2nd Century BCE)
Astika - Naturalist, Atomist

### Core Doctrine
Reality is analyzed in 7 categories
- Substance, quality, Action, universality, particularity, inherence and non-existence
- Matter is Made up of atoms
- Methods: [[Perception]] and [[Inference]]

Wester Counterpart : Greek Atomism
