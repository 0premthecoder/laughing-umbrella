# Fixes that Fail

- **Mechanism:** A quick, symptomatic solution provides short-term relief, but sets off delayed unintended consequences that exacerbate the original problem and create dependency on the fix.
    
- **Example:** Kodak prioritized traditional film marketing and cost-cutting over cannibalizing its business with digital photography, leading to bankruptcy.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Focus on root causes rather than symptoms; resist the urgency of quick fixes.
