# Monopoly  vs Competition

|**Feature**|**Monopoly**|**Competitive Firm**|
|---|---|---|
|**Number of Producers**|Is the sole producer|Is one of many producers|
|**Demand Curve**|Faces a downward-sloping demand curve|Faces a horizontal demand curve|
|**Price Control**|Is a price maker|Is a price taker|
|**Sales & Pricing Strategy**|Can reduce its sales to increase price|Sells as much or as little as it wants at market price|
