# Left Brain vs Right Brain

|**Left Brain**|**Right Brain**|
|---|---|
|Uses logic and structure|Uses feelings and “big-picture” framing|
|Detail-oriented; prizes facts|Imagination dominant; symbols and images|
|Words, language, math, science|Spatial perception and aesthetics|
|Order and pattern recognition|Fantasy-based; presents possibilities|
|Reality-based; forms strategies|Impetuous, risk-taking, generative|
|Practical, safe, deductive|Strong in pattern leaps and metaphor|
