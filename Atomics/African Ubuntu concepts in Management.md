# African Ubuntu concepts in Management

| **Concept**                  | **Core Meaning**                                               | **Management Translation**                                       |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu** | Relational personhood ("a person is a person through others")  | Organization as a relational community, not isolated individuals |
| **Solidarity**               | Mutual support and shared collective fate                      | Collective problem-solving over purely individual metrics        |
| **Compassion**               | Active concern for wellbeing as an organizational duty         | Welfare embedded in core HR/governance (not optional CSR)        |
| **Respect and dignity**      | Inherent dignity and voice for every person regardless of rank | Participatory, cross-hierarchical inclusive decision-making      |
| **Firm as community**        | Promoting the common good of all stakeholders                  | Stakeholder-inclusive governance (e.g., King Reports)            |
