# Leibnizian System

The Leibnizian Inquiring System draws its lineage from the Continental
Rationalists - 
Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (1646-1716), 
René Descartes (1596-1650), 
and Benedict de Spinoza (1632-1677).

Rationalism is a way to Approach Reality Irrespective of Our senses

Churchman's Rendering
- Construct a Model or Theory first
- Data is then fed into Model to generate Output
- Then Observe theory

System ask: **"Given our Model, What must be true?"**

Most powerful when assumptions are valid and the problem is well-bounded
Hallmark question: "How was the result deduced, and is it precise and certain?"

The Leibnizian tradition gave management science its analytical backbone. 
like [[Frederick Winslow Taylor (Scientific Management)]]
