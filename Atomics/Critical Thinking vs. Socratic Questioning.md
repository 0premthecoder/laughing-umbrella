# Critical Thinking vs. Socratic Questioning

- **Socratic Questioning:** Inherently **dialogic**—it requires two or more parties engaged in disciplined, reflective conversation to uncover truth.
    
- **Critical Thinking (CT):** Encompasses both dialogic interactions and individual reflective analysis. It functions as both a solo cognitive practice (e.g., a manager auditing a report) and an institutionalized organizational workflow.
    
- **Core Definition:** Reflective, evidence-sensitive judgment used to evaluate whether a strategic claim is **true enough, relevant enough, and useful enough** to guide capital allocation and operational action.
