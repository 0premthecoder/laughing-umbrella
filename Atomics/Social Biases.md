# Social Biases

- **Source:** Peer pressure, organizational hierarchy, and cultural norms.
    
- **Risk:** Collective mistakes, suppressed dissent, and poor strategic alignment.
    
- **Key Types:**
    
    - **Groupthink:** Suppressing dissent and critical evaluation to maintain harmony and unanimity within a group.
        
    - **Authority Bias:** Overvaluing opinions or strategy decisions solely due to the power or executive rank of the person proposing them.
        
    - **Herding (Bandwagon Effect):** Adopting strategies purely because competitors or industry peers are doing so (e.g., investing in tech strictly because "everyone else is").
        
    - **In-Group Bias:** Favorably evaluating or promoting individuals who share similar backgrounds, universities, or identity traits.
        
    - **Social Proof Bias:** Validating an operational choice because many high-profile peers endorse it rather than running an independent internal assessment.
