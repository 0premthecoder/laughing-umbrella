# 5 enemies of Rational Thought

[[Self-Deception]]

[[Cognitive Distortions]]

[[Groupthink]]

[[Obedience to Authority]]

[[Fixed Mindset]]

|**Enemy**|**Core Flaw**|**Organizational Risk**|**Primary Remedy**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Self-Deception**|Refusing to accept uncomfortable truths|Strategic denial & delayed action|Decision journals & external benchmarking|
|**Cognitive Distortion**|Irrational interpretation of facts|Incorrect root-cause diagnosis|Structured reasoning & alternative hypotheses|
|**Groupthink**|Forcing consensus over critical thought|Collective strategic blunders|Red teaming & designed dissent|
|**Obedience to Authority**|Rank replacing objective evidence|Unchallenged top-down errors|Authority-neutral decision procedures|
|**Fixed Mindset**|Treating capabilities as permanent|Inability to adapt or innovate|Growth culture & after-action reviews|
