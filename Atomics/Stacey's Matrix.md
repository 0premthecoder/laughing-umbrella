# Stacey's Matrix

Ralph Douglas Stacey, Professor of Management at the University of
Hertfordshire Business School, developed what came to be known as Stacey's
Matrix in the mid-1990s.

It argues that strategy is not a plan but a pattern in a stream of decisions - and
that most significant strategic decisions are made in conditions of genuine
uncertainty and disagreement.
