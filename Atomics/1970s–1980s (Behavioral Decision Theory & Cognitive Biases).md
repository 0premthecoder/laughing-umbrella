# 1970s–1980s (Behavioral Decision Theory & Cognitive Biases)

- **Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky:** Demonstrated systematic, predictable departures from rationality caused by heuristics (mental shortcuts).
	
- **Cognitive Modes:** System 1 (fast, automatic, intuitive) vs. System 2 (slow, deliberate, analytical).
	
- **Prospect Theory & Loss Aversion:** Losses loom larger than equivalent gains. Explains reluctance to divest failing units, sunk cost fallacies, and escalation of commitment (e.g., prolonged Indian PSU bailouts, Tata Nano exit delay).
