# Escalation

- **Mechanism:** Competing actors perceive each other's moves as threats and respond with retaliatory actions, creating a reinforcing cycle of mutual threat that raises costs and risks for everyone.
    
- **Example:** Amazon undercut Quidsy's diaper pricing to neutralize competition, leveraging deep pockets until Quidsy's position became untenable and it was acquired.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Break the cycle through unilateral deactivation or negotiated mutual de-escalation.
