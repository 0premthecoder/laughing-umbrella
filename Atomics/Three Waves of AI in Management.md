# Three Waves of AI in Management

- **Wave 1: Analytical AI (1990–2015):** Statistical models and early ML for customer segmentation, churn, forecasting, risk (e.g., Amazon recommendations, Netflix personalization, Google PageRank, HDFC underwriting, Flipkart forecasting).
    
- **Wave 2: Perceptual AI (2012–2022):** Deep learning revolution in vision, NLP, and speech (e.g., AlexNet, AlphaFold, medical imaging, customer chatbots, automated document processing).
    
- **Wave 3: Generative AI (2022–Present):** LLMs and multi-modal models with emergent capabilities (multi-step reasoning, code debugging, research synthesis). Shifted competitive advantage from _information access_ to _sensemaking quality_.
