# Yoga School

Founder: Patanjali, Yoga Sutra (2nd BCE-4th BCE) 
Astika - theistic Samkhya ([[Samkhya School]] with personal god)

### Core Doctrine

- Goal is the cessation of Fluctuation of mind, so Purusha may rest in its true nature
- Achieved through 8 limbs (Ashtanga Yoga), Ethical Restrains (Yama), self-discipline (niyama), Posture (Asana), breath-control (pranayama), sense-withdrawal (pratyahara), concentration (Dharana), Meditation (Dhayana), Absorption (Samadhi)

Method: Direct Cultivated Experience from practices

Western Counterpart: Stoicism
