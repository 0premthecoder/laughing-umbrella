# Systems Archetypes

- **Definition:** Recurring patterns of system behavior that link underlying structures to behavior over time.
    
- **Architecture:** Composed of stable configurations of reinforcing loops, balancing loops, and characteristic delays.
    
- **Core Functions (Daniel Kim):** Act as lenses revealing causal patterns, structural pattern templates for organizational situations, dynamic scripts for system narratives, and tools for predicting behavioral outcomes and unintended consequences.
