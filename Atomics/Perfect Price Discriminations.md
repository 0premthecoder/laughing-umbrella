# Perfect Price Discriminations

Perfect price discrimination refers to the situation when
the monopolist knows each customer's willingness to pay, and
can charge each customer exactly what he/she is willing to pay.

Example
- Swiggy knows you order late at night and live far from restaurants. 
- It infers you're willing to pay more and charges a higher delivery fee.
- A nearby customer who orders at noon pays less for the same meal.

The firm captures all consumer surplus.
No deadweight loss - but buyers are no better off than if they didn't buy.
