# Diseconomies of Scale

Long Run Average total cost rises as the quantity of Output increases

Increasing Coordination Problems in large Organization 
e.g Management streched, can't control cost, more common in higher Q

### Reasons
Grow production workers
10× more workers means 10x more output - 
but not 10x more total inputs.

Supervision can't scale linearly
Each new layer of workers requires a new layer of
supervisors and managers above them.

The only alternative?
Reduce supervision intensity - but this directly reduces
productivity per worker.
