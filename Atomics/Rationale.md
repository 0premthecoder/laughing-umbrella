# Rationale

Critical Thinking (CT) and Creative Problem Solving (CPS) are complementary modes of thought within managerial decision-making,

[[Critical Thinking (CT)]]

[[Creative Problem Solving (CPS)]]

| **Dimension**       | **Critical Thinking (CT)**                | **Creative Problem Solving (CPS)**            |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Focus**   | Evaluation & Quality Control              | Generation & Innovation                       |
| **Cognitive Goal**  | Filter out defective conclusions          | Expand the range of potential solutions       |
| **Core Mechanism**  | System 2 (Analytical)                     | Hybrid System 1 & System 2                    |
| **Judgment Timing** | Active during framing, selection, & audit | **Deferred** during initial option generation |
| **Primary Danger**  | Over-analysis and premature rejection     | Unvetted ideas that fail in execution         |
