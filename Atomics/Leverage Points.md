# Leverage Points

By Donella Meadows (Thinking in System (2008))

- **Definition:** Specific points in a system's structure where a small, well-placed change can produce a large, system-wide shift (derived from Archimedes' lever, fulcrum, and weight analogy).
    
- **Core Problem:** Managers and policymakers frequently target visible, tangible, and quantifiable elements (like numbers and parameters) while ignoring deeply influential structural and paradigm layers.
