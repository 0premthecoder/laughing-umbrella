# Welfare in monopolistic Competition

Not All Welfare Properties Met
- markets lack the desirable welfare properties of [[Characteristics of Perfect Competitive Market|Perfectly Competitive Market]]

Quantity Below Social Optimum
- Because P > MC, 
- some consumers who value the good above its production cost are priced out
- Mutually beneficial trades go unmade.

Deadweight Loss Exists
- The gap between price and marginal cost creates a deadweight loss 

It can't be fixed because Economic Profit = 0
Price cut = Losses = Exit from Market

We know Firms aren't Producing in Optimum
