# Person & Deflection Fallacies

- **Ad Hominem:** Attacking the person making the argument rather than evaluating the logic itself (e.g., dismissing an analyst's valuation because they are "too young").
    
- **Red Herring:** Distracting from the core issue by introducing an irrelevant topic (e.g., pointing to higher customer satisfaction scores when asked why profits fell).
    
- **Loaded Question:** Asking a question embedded with an unjustified, controversial assumption so that any direct answer implies guilt (e.g., _"Why does marketing keep wasting money on failed campaigns?"_).
