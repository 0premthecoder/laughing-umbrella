# Tools for Restricting Trade

Tariff: A tax on imports that lead to trade Restriction

[[Consequences of Tariff]]

An **Import Quota** is a quantitative limit on imports of a good
has the same effects as Tarrif

though 
A tariff = Profit for Govt
An Import Quota = Profit for Foreign Producers
- Govt could auction license to import to capture this profit as revenue
