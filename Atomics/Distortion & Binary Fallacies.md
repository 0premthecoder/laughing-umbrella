# Distortion & Binary Fallacies

- **Straw Person:** Misrepresenting or exaggerating an opponent's argument to make it easier to refute (e.g., framing a proposal to automate small tasks as _"trying to eliminate all human jobs"_).
    
- **False Dilemma (False Binary):** Framing a complex situation as having only two extreme choices when multiple strategic alternatives exist (e.g., _"Either we cut 20% of staff or the company fails"_).
    
- **Slippery Slope:** Claiming without evidence that a minor initial step will inevitably cause an extreme chain reaction (e.g., asserting that allowing remote work one day a week will collapse corporate culture).
    
- **Nirvana (Perfect Solution) Fallacy:** Rejecting a viable real-world improvement simply because it does not achieve absolute perfection (e.g., refusing an IoT maintenance system because it cannot stop _every_ machine breakdown).
