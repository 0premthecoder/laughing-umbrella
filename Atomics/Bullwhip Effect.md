# Bullwhip Effect

A minor, isolated variance in downstream retail demand gets progressively amplified as it moves upstream through distributors, warehouses, and component suppliers, resulting in massive inventory spikes or stockouts.

```
[Retailer Demand Spike (+5%)] ──> [Distributor Order (+15%)] ──> [Manufacturer Production (+50%)]
```

- **Systemic Interventions:** Share real-time Point-of-Sale (POS) data across all supply chain tiers, reduce batch sizes, align supplier incentives, and stabilize promotional pricing.
