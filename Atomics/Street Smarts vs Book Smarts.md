# Street Smarts vs Book Smarts

Book smarts are Trained for Classroom problems which are well-defined but Real world problem are ill-defined and the answers are not clear cut single answer but the answer relies on managerial Judgement and will have to bear the bad consequences if it occurs due to wrong choice

Classroom information is mostly complete, while managerial information is missing, ambiguous, or politically contested.
