# Stock and Flow Diagram

A **stock and flow diagram** is a visual modeling tool used in system dynamics to analyze how variables accumulate or deplete within a system over time.


| **Concept**            | **Definition**                                                      | **Business Examples**                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stock**              | An accumulation that carries memory from the past into the present  | Inventory, cash balance, customer base, installed base, employee experience, brand equity |
| **Inflow**             | A rate that increases a stock                                       | Production, hiring, customer acquisition, cash inflow, capability building                |
| **Outflow**            | A rate that decreases a stock                                       | Shipments, attrition, customer churn, depreciation, defects leaving inspection            |
| **Auxiliary variable** | A converter that influences flows but is not itself an accumulation | Marketing spend, price, service quality, lead time, training intensity                    |
| **Delay**              | A lag between cause and effect                                      | Hiring-to-productivity delay, capacity expansion delay, brand-building delay              |
