# Astika-Nastika

Each Astika school is a rigorous darshana - literally a "way of seeing" or a school of
systematic philosophical vision - with its own founding sutra-text, commentarial tradition, and
theory of valid knowledge.

Heterodox schools built independent epistemologies and, in the case of Buddhism and
Jainism, elaborate ethical and metaphysical systems without reference to Vedic revelation.
