# Socratic Inquiry Steps

Step-by-Step Socratic Workshop Process (30–60 Min)


| **Step**                               | **Action & Key Focus**                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Frame Decision as a Question**    | **Shift from Advocacy Mode $\rightarrow$ Inquiry Mode**<br><br>  <br><br>• _Weak:_ "Should we acquire Company X?"<br><br>  <br><br>• _Strong:_ "What is our most value-accretive growth path?" |
| **2. Seek Clarification**              | Define success metrics, terms, and timeline parameters                                                                                                                                         |
| **3. Probe Assumptions**               | Explicitly map PESTEL macro, customer, and internal assumptions                                                                                                                                |
| **4. Demand Evidence**                 | Distinguish quantified data from interpretations and opinions                                                                                                                                  |
| **5. Explore Implications**            | Map 1st and 2nd-order downstream strategic/reputational effects                                                                                                                                |
| **6. Invite Alternative Perspectives** | Assign specific team members as Devil's Advocate and Customer Voice                                                                                                                            |
| **7. Question the Question**           | Re-evaluate problem framing based on the dialogue's findings                                                                                                                                   |
