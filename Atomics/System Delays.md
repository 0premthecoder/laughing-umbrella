# System Delays

Delays separate cause and effect in time and space, making system diagnosis counter-intuitive and triggering erratic oscillations.

- **Perception Delay:** Time taken to recognize a market shift (e.g., Kodak recognizing digital photography as a fatal threat too late).
    
- **Response Delay:** Time required to plan and execute a strategic response (e.g., 3–5 years required to build and staff a new semiconductor fabrication facility).
    
- **Delivery Delay:** Physical or logistical lag in moving goods, often driving hoarding behavior.
    
- **Information Delay:** Lag caused by reliance on periodic rather than real-time data (e.g., waiting for 90-day quarterly financial reports instead of real-time operational dashboards).
    
- **Learning Delay:** Time required for new recruits or new organizational capabilities to reach peak productivity.
