# Drifting Goals

- **Mechanism:** When a performance gap exists, organizations gradually lower performance standards to match current results rather than raising capabilities, leading to long-term decline.
    
- **Example:** General Motors systematically lowered its strategic goals over decades from market dominance to profitability in core segments, leading to its 2009 bankruptcy.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Make original goals explicit, visible, and institutionally protected; make goal reductions politically and publicly costly.
