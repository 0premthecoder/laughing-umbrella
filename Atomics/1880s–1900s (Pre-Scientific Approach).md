# 1880s–1900s (Pre-Scientific Approach)

- Decision-making based on intuition, tradition, precedent, and personal authority of top proprietors.
	
- Information flowed via trust-based networks rather than formalized reporting (e.g., East India Company, Marwari trading networks).
	
- **Virtues:** Fast, contextually sensitive, rooted in genuine expertise.
	
- **Weaknesses:** Opaque, hard to transfer, vulnerable to personal biases.
