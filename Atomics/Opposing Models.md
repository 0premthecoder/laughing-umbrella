# Opposing Models

These are models that differ each other for Conventional thinker they see it as conflict 
for Integrated thinkers they see it as Complement

|**Opposing Models**|**Key Trade-off**|
|---|---|
|**Centralization vs. Decentralization**|Tight control & consistency vs. local responsiveness & autonomy|
|**Low Cost vs. High Differentiation**|Porter's cost leadership vs. unique premium value (integrative goal: both)|
|**Short-Term Results vs. Long-Term Investment**|Quarterly earnings discipline vs. patient capital for innovation|
|**Standardization vs. Customization**|Operational efficiency vs. customer intimacy|
|**Individual Performance vs. Team Collaboration**|Clear accountability & incentives vs. collective capability|
|**Innovation vs. Execution**|Creative disruption vs. disciplined implementation|
|**Growth vs. Profitability**|Market share expansion vs. return on invested capital (ROIC)|
