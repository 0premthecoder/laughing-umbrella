# Four Causes

It is used for Analytical Purpose in Organisation

|**Cause (Latin)**|**Core Question**|**Statue Example**|**Business Example**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Material** _(Causa Materialis)_|What is it made of?|Marble stone|People, money, tech, raw resources|
|**Formal** _(Causa Formalis)_|What is its design?|Shape/blueprint|Org structure, strategy, business model|
|**Efficient** _(Causa Efficiens)_|Who or what made it?|Sculptor & chisel|Founder, CEO, change agents|
|**Final** _(Causa Finalis)_|Why does it exist?|Honor a god / decorate|Mission, purpose, stakeholder value|
