# Limits to Success

- **Mechanism:** Initial growth driven by a reinforcing loop eventually hits an internal or external constraint (capacity, saturation, quality degradation) that slows progress despite intensified effort.
    
- **Example:** Starbucks experienced rapid store growth and revenue increases until hitting U.S. market saturation, brand dilution, and degraded customer experience from rapid barista hiring.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Identify and address the binding constraint directly rather than pushing harder on the growth driver.
