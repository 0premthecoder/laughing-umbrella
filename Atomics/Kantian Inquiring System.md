# Kantian Inquiring System

Immanuel Kant (1724-1804) attempted the great synthesis of rationalism and empiricism in
his Critique of Pure Reason (1781).

"Concepts without intuitions are empty; intuitions without Concepts are blind."

In Churchman's framework, the Kantian Inquiring System holds data and theory as co-equal
and inseparable.

- Truth is synthetic
- Theory and data are inseparable; neither has priority over the other
- A multi-model, multi-perspective approach generate many explicit views; the best fitting synthesis is the answer

Produces rich, Multi-Dimensional understanding of complex problems
