# 3 Essential Elements of Any System (Meadows)

1. **Elements:** The visible/tangible or conceptual parts (e.g., people, machines, capital, ideas).
    
2. **Interconnections:** The relationships, rules, physical flows, and information links binding the elements together.
    
3. **Function or Purpose:** What the system actually _does_ (often revealed by its actual behavior and outputs, rather than its stated intent).
    

- **Business Example (Amazon's Order Fulfillment):**
    
    - _Elements:_ Warehouses, Kiva robots, delivery fleets, software algorithms, human workers.
        
    - _Interconnections:_ Demand signals, inventory algorithms, delivery routing, APIs.
        
    - _Purpose:_ To deliver goods faster and cheaper than any competitor.
