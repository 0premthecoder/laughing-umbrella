# Buddhism

Founder: Sidharth Gautama, the buddha (5th Century BCE)

Nastika - Process oriented, Anti-substantialist

### Core Doctrine 

- Four Nobel Truths, 
	- Existence pervaded by suffering 
	- Caused by Cravings
	- curable by its cessation
	- treated by eight fold path
- Distinguished Doctrines 
	- Annica (all phenomenon are impermanent)
	- Anatta (there is no unchanging self)
		- The person is causally linked, ever changing stream of aggregrates (skandas)
			- arises through dependent origination (pratityasamutpada)

The middle way rejects both eternalism and nihilism 
Knowledge is good only when it can remove suffering, not for its own sake
