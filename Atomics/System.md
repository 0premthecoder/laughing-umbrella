# System

Derived from the Greek _systema_ ("a whole compounded of several parts"). 
A system is a complex of interacting, interdependent elements forming a purposeful, behavioral whole

**Key Thinkers' Perspectives:**

- _Ludwig von Bertalanffy:_ A complex of interacting elements.
    
- _Peter Checkland:_ A whole entity capable of purposeful human activity.
    
- _Peter Senge:_ A group of interrelated elements forming a complex whole.
    
- _Donella Meadows:_ A set of elements, interconnections, and a function/purpose.
    
- _John Sterman:_ Interrelated elements that together produce specific _behavior_.
