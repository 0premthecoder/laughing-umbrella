# Measures to compute GDP

**Income Approach**
- add all components of income arrive at GDP

**Value added Approach**
- Amount Rupee value is added during production of goods 


**Expenditure Approach**
- Measures GDP by adding all the Price value of Good & Services

GDP = C + I + G + NX 

C = Consumer Spent money

I = Investment Purchase Price
- Business Fixed Investment
- New Housing
- Inventory Purchases
- Not small stock market purchases

G = Govt Purchasing
- Goods and Service Purchased by Govt + Salaries give 
- Not Transfer Payments (Pensions, Scheme etc)

Nx = (Exports - Import)

Excluded Items from GDP
- buying and selling used goods
- Stock market transaction
- cash gifts between individuals
- Transfer Payements
- illegal transaction
