# Creative Problem Solving (CPS)

- **Role:** The "innovation generator." Focuses on expanding the solution space, exploring possibilities, and reframing problems.
    
- **Purpose:** Produces novel options and fresh frames of thought without premature judgment.
    
- **Cognitive Mode:** Recruits and integrates both **System 1** (associative, pattern-breaking) and **System 2** (structured techniques to harness intuition and analogy).
    
- **Evolution:** Traced from Graham Wallas (_The Art of Thought_) $\rightarrow$ Alex Osborn (_Applied Imagination_) $\rightarrow$ Edward de Bono (Lateral Thinking, Six Hats) $\rightarrow$ IDEO (Design Thinking movement
