# Human-AI Traps & Countermeasures

- _Automation Bias (Overtrust):_ Independent human baseline checks, confidence/uncertainty scoring.
        
- _Algorithm Aversion (Undertrust from rare errors):_ Calibrated baseline comparisons, bounded overrides.
	
- _Deskilling:_ Practice rotations, failure simulations, manual review retention.
	
- _Responsibility Gap:_ Explicit named accountability across business, data, and risk owners.
	
- _Local Productivity Illusion:_ Measuring end-to-end value system impact rather than isolated task speed.
