# Management Implications & Meta-Skills

- **Avoid Strategic Mistiming:**
    
    - _Evaluating too early:_ Destroys divergent ideas before they mature.
        
    - _Generating too late:_ Wastes resources after capital commitments are already locked in.
        
- **Integrative Thinking & Phronesis (Practical Wisdom):**
    
    - Conceptualized by Roger Martin (_Opposable Minds_), integrative thinking requires managers to hold conflicting ideas simultaneously rather than choosing binary options (_"Quality OR Productivity"_ → _"Quality AND Productivity"_).
        
    - **Phronesis:** The meta-skill of knowing precisely when a decision moment calls for analytical evaluation (CT) versus generative exploration (CPS), and maintaining the discipline to stay in that mode.
