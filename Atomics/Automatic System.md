# Automatic System

Fast, Low effort, often Subconscious
- Feels Instinctive (not thinking)
- Always on
- Lead to Cognitive bias

### Strengths
- **Speed:** Enables quick decisions in uncertain environments.
- **Pattern Recognition:** Allows experts to recognize familiar situations rapidly.
- **Efficiency:** Helps managers handle hundreds of routine decisions.

### Weaknesses
- Highly vulnerable to:
    - Biases
    - Stereotypes
    - Emotional reactions
    - Overconfidence
