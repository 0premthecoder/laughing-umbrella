|Tool|Best Used When...|Core Mechanism / Action|Primary Caution / Risk|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Dunker Diagram**|Stuck between current and desired states|Maps hierarchical routes from goal to concrete leaf solutions|Don't over-develop one branch too early|
|**Statement / Restatement**|Problem statement sounds narrow, biased, or complaint-driven|Generates 5–8 alternative formulations using inversion or analogies|Avoid semantic wordplay without strategic substance|
|**Reframing**|Trapped in legacy industry assumptions or functional blame|Changes the mental model (Stakeholder, Pain-point, Competitor lens)|Re-framed model must remain empirically testable|
|**Lateral Thinking**|Incremental ideas dominate board discussions|Uses random entry, provocations (PO), and structural challenges|Provocation is a generative tool, not proof of feasibility|
|**Brainstorming / Variants**|Seeking a massive volume of diverse, recombined options|Defer judgment, build via "Yes, and...", or use Brainwriting (6-3-5)|Unstructured verbal sessions suffer from production blocking|
|**SCAMPER**|An existing product, service, or process needs systematic variation|Runs focal object through 7 structural prompts (Substitute → Reverse)|Always tie output back to customer value and feasibility|
|**Futuring & Backcasting**|Decisions face high lead times, high turbulence, or macro shifts|Builds 2x2 scenario matrices or works backward from a target future state|Scenarios are strategic stress-tests, not predictive forecasts|
