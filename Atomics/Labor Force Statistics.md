# Labor Force Statistics

Produced by Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Govt of India - via PLFS (Periodic Labour Force Survey)

Based on Adult Population (15 yrs or Older)

[[Division of Employment]]

Unemployment Rate ("u-rate")

u-rate = 100 x $\text{no of unemployed} \over \text{labour force}$

Labour Force = People willing to Be Employed and Actively Searching for jobs

Labour force Participation Rate

=  100 x $\text{labour force} \over \text{15+ age Population}$


There are limitation on U-rate
- Can't measure the Effective workers (Some more train working for a lower paying job)
- Discouraged workers are classified as Not in Labour Force rather than Unemployed
