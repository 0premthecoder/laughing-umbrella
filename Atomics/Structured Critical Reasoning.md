# Structured Critical Reasoning

- **Definition:** The practice of making the underlying structure of reasoning fully visible, transparent, and decision-oriented.
    
- **Core Function:** Unlike decisions driven by authority or intuition, SCR structures complex problems so that analysis is **complete, non-overlapping, and decision-driven**.
    
- **Position in Frameworks:** Represents the most formalized and analytically rigorous step in the reasoning spectrum, evolving from Socratic Questioning (dialogic) and Critical Thinking (reflective/evaluative).
