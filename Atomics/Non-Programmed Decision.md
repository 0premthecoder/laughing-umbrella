# Non-Programmed Decision

Decisions arises in  
- Complex
- Important
- & non-routine situations
- Unfamiliar places

The decisions lacks some procedures and demands creative problem solving

The conventional toolkit of rational analysis is necessary but insufficient: one must
also generate genuinely new alternatives - alternatives not derivable from precedent.

This is precisely where Integrative Thinking offers distinctive value.
