# How to behave in VUCA

| **Dimension**   | **Key Dilemma / Question**                          | **Diagnostic Misstep to Avoid**                                    | **Prescribed Managerial Behavior**                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Volatility**  | _How fast and how much will conditions change?_     | Underreacting or overreacting to turbulence.                       | • Build agile execution routines and slack resources.<br><br>  <br><br>• Maintain buffers (e.g., strategic inventory, talent bench).<br><br>  <br><br>• Preserve flexibility and short response times.       |
| **Uncertainty** | _What might happen, and what do we need to learn?_  | Pretending precision where only broad scenarios exist.             | • Invest in gathering and interpreting information.<br><br>  <br><br>• Use scenario planning instead of single-point forecasting.<br><br>  <br><br>• Make staged commitments and form information alliances. |
| **Complexity**  | _How do interacting parts produce system outcomes?_ | Local optimization (optimizing one part while damaging the whole). | • Map interdependencies using systems thinking.<br><br>  <br><br>• Bring in specialists and restructure for alignment.<br><br>  <br><br>• Coordinate stakeholders and monitor feedback loops.                |
| **Ambiguity**   | _What does this mean when precedents are weak?_     | Forcing legacy categories/models onto novel problems.              | • Apply abductive reasoning and sensemaking frameworks.<br><br>  <br><br>• Formulate and test hypotheses through structured business experimentation.<br><br>  <br><br>• Run low-cost pilots before scaling. |
