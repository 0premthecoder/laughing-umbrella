# Dunning Kurger Effect

![[Pasted image 20260723113248.png|270]]

**"Mount Stupid"**
A small amount of knowledge produces a
large increase in confidence.
People think: "I understand this completely."

**Valley of Despair**
As deeper complexities become visible,
corfidence collapses.
Peuple realize: "This is far more complicated
than I thought."

**Slope of Enlightenment**
Knowledge gradually increases.
Confidence becomes calibrated.

**Plateau of Sustainability**
Experts possess substantial knowledge but
also appreciate uncertainty and limitations.
