# Profit Maximizing on Monopoly

For any firm Profit Maximizing quantity only occur when 
Marginal Revenue = Marginal Cost

In Equilibrium, Quantity Supplied = Quantity Demanded

A monopoly firm then uses the demand curve to find the price
that will induce consumers to buy the profit-maximizing
quantity.

![[Pasted image 20260803084700.png|386]]
- **Pricing:** Move up from QMAX​ to the **Demand curve** (Point B) to set price PM​.
    
- **Key Inequality:** **P>MR=MC** (Price exceeds marginal cost, creating a markup).
    
- **Vs. Perfect Competition:** Monopoly charges a **higher price** and produces a **lower quantity** than competitive markets (P=MC).
