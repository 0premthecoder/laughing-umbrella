# Right Brain and Left Brain Thinking in Business

Left Brain Style is about
- Data
- Logic
- Analysis
- Optimization
- Risk Assessment
>What does number say?, What is ROI?, What is the evidence?

Right Brain Style is about
- Vision
- Creativity
- Possibilities
- Storytelling
- Pattern Recognition
>What could be?, What are feelings of Customers?, What are new Oppurtunities?
