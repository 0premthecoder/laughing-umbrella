# 5 Core Disciplines

| **Discipline**          | **Core Dimension**   | **Primary Focus**                                                                                         | **Corporate Illustration**                                                                                      |
| ----------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Personal Mastery** | Individual           | Continually clarifying personal vision, developing purpose, and maintaining an objective self-assessment. | **Google:** Allocating 20% time for employees to develop personal mastery in emerging tech/projects.            |
| **2. Mental Models**    | Cognitive / Cultural | Surfacing, auditing, and refining internal worldviews that govern strategic choices.                      | **Microsoft:** Satya Nadella shifting culture from _"Windows-centric control"_ to _"Cloud-first, open-source."_ |
| **3. Shared Vision**    | Organizational       | Building genuine collective ownership of a future state rather than top-down compliance.                  | **Tata Group:** Group-wide commitment across 80+ companies toward community quality-of-life.                    |
| **4. Team Learning**    | Collaborative        | Transforming team conversations from defensive debate into generative, vulnerable dialogue.               | **Pixar:** The _Braintrust_ forum, where directors receive non-binding, candid peer critiques.                  |
| **5. Systems Thinking** | **Meta-Discipline**  | Integrating all disciplines to see wholes, feedback architecture, delays, and leverage points.            | **Amazon:** Jeff Bezos mapping the customer experience _Flywheel_ to align every business unit.                 |
