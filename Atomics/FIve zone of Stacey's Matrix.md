# FIve zone of Stacey's Matrix

|**Matrix Position**|**Management Zone**|**Core Approach**|**Business Example**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**High Certainty + High Agreement**|**Simple / Rational**|SOPs, classical planning, standards|Annual budgeting, factory SOPs|
|**Low Certainty + High Agreement**|**Complicated**|Technical expertise & analytical benchmarking|Infrastructure investments, supply chain optimization|
|**High Certainty + Low Agreement**|**Political**|Negotiation & coalition building|Post-merger integration, board governance disputes|
|**Low Certainty + Low Agreement**|**Complex (Creative)**|Emergence, experimentation, minimal structure|New ventures, digital transformation|
|**Extreme Lows (Anarchy)**|**Chaotic**|Crisis intervention & rapid stabilization|Regulatory collapse, black swan events|
