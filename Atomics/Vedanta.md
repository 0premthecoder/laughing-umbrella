# Vedanta

Founder: Badarayana: Brahma Sutra (2nd Century BCE-2nd Century CE)
Astika - Metaphysical : Branches into four principles

### Core Doctrine

Brahma (Ultimate Reality) and Atman (Self) are central object of inquiry

Advaita (Shankara) - Brahma = Atman, world = maya

Vishishtadvaita (Ramanuja) - Souls and matter are real modes of one Brahman

Dvaita (Madhva) - God, Souls and matter are enternally and irreductibly distinct

Neo-Vedanta (Vivekananda) - Modern Practical and ethical View on Vedas

Scriptural testimony (shruti) interpreted through reasoning (yukti) and confirmed by direct realization (anubhav)

### Western Counterpart
Advaita parallels Neoplatonism
Vishishtadvaita parallels panentheism and aspects of Whiteheadian
Dvaita parallels Western theistic personalism
Neo-Vedanta parallels the Transcendentalism of Emerson and Thoreau (Translation of Upanishada)
