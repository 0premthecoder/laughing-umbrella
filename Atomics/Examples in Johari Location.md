# Examples in Johari Location

| **Thinking Problem**   | **Johari Location**      | **Example**                                               | **Remedy**                  |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Confirmation bias      | Blind Area               | CEO sees only evidence supporting preferred strategy      | Seek disconfirming evidence |
| Overconfidence         | Blind Area               | Founder overestimates market readiness                    | Base-rate analysis          |
| Groupthink             | Hidden Area + Blind Area | People privately disagree but publicly comply             | Anonymous dissent           |
| Appeal to authority    | Blind Area               | Team accepts CEO's view without evidence                  | Authority-neutral review    |
| False dilemma          | Blind Area               | "Cut staff or fail"                                       | Generate alternatives       |
| Thinking frame lock-in | Blind Area               | Firm sees itself as product company, not service platform | Reframe the business        |
| Suppressed bad news    | Hidden Area              | Employees know project is failing but stay silent         | Psychological safety        |
| Unseen opportunity     | Unknown Area             | Manufacturer discovers service revenue model              | Experimentat                |
