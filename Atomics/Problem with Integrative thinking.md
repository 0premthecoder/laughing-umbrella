# Problem with Integrative thinking

- **Selection / Survivorship Bias:** Researched only on successful firms/leaders; ignores failed attempts, making true reliability unproven.
    
- **Execution Complexity:** Sound concepts often fail during value-chain implementation (e.g., Tata Nano); focuses on strategy at the top, not frontline execution capability.
    
- **High Cognitive Load in Crisis:** Demands heavy mental bandwidth, which collapses under high stress and time pressure where routine/recognition-primed models work better.
    
- **Paradox of Creative Synthesis:** Not all trade-offs are false dichotomies; refusing to pick a side when real trade-offs exist results in achieving neither objective.
