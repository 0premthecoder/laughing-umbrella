# Fixed Mindset

- **Core Mechanism:** The belief that intelligence, capability, and talent (individual or organizational) are innate and unchangeable.
    
- **Fixed vs. Growth Posture:**
    
    - _Handling Failure:_ Fixed says _"We aren't good at this"_; Growth asks _"What capability are we missing and how can we build it?"_
        
    - _Handling Criticism:_ Fixed sees an attack; Growth sees a learning opportunity.
        
    - _Handling Competitors:_ Fixed assumes _"They were lucky"_; Growth asks _"What are they doing better?"_
        
- **Remedies:** After-action reviews, capability academies, mentoring-oriented performance reviews, failure analysis without personal blame, and stretch assignments.
