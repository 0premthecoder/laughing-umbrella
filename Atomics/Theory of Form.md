# Theory of Form

[[Allegory of the Cave]] is the metaphor of Theory of Form

Plat argues the world we percieves is imperfect and the real perfect lies in Form (ideas & essence) 
- they are eternal 

3 Propositions

Reality is hierarchical
- Abstract Forms
- Physical Objects
- Images & Appereances

Knowledge vs Opinion
- True Knowledge (Episteme) is of the Form - Stable, Certain, Rational
- Opinion (Doxa) - is of the changing physical world - probable, subject to revision & sense-dependent

The Form of good is highest form
- just like sun makes every object visible
- The form of good is source of being & knowability of all other form
