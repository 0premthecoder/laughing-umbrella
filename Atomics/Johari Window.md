# Johari Window

**Johari Window** is a psychological framework developed in 1955 by Joseph Luft and Harry Ingham. It helps individuals understand and improve self-awareness, personal development, and group dynamics

![[Pasted image 20260723191433.png|212]]

- **Open Area (Arena):** Information, behaviors, and feelings known to both the individual and others.
    
- **Blind Spot:** Aspects of an individual that others observe, but the individual is unaware of.
    
- **Hidden Area (Facade):** Information kept private by the individual and hidden from others.
    
- **Unknown Area:** Thoughts, feelings, talents, or behaviors unknown to both the individual and others.
