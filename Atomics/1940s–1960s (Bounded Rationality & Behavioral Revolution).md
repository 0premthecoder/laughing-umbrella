# 1940s–1960s (Bounded Rationality & Behavioral Revolution)

- **Herbert Simon:** Disproved classical assumptions of perfect information, consistent preferences, and unlimited cognitive power.
        
- **Core Concept:** Decision makers face constraints on cognitive ability, information, and time.
	
- **Satisficing:** Selecting a "good enough" (satisfactory + sufficient) alternative rather than optimizing. Organizations establish SOPs and hierarchies to offset individual cognitive limits.
	
- **Cyert & March (_A Behavioral Theory of the Firm_):** Organizational choices emerge from group bargaining and coalitions, not a single unitary objective.
