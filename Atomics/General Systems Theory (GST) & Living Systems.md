# General Systems Theory (GST) & Living Systems

Pioneered by **Ludwig von Bertalanffy**, GST establishes that similar structural principles govern systems across completely different domains (**Isomorphism**).

- **Isomorphism:** Applying structural models across fields (e.g., modeling market share battles in oligopolistic industries using biological _Predator-Prey cycles_).
    
- **Entropy:** The physical/organizational measure of disorder. Left unmanaged, organizations experience _calcification_, bureaucratic inertia, and institutional decay.
    
- **Living Systems Theory (James Miller):** Outlines 8 hierarchical system levels: _Cell $\rightarrow$ Organ $\rightarrow$ Organism $\rightarrow$ Group $\rightarrow$ Organization $\rightarrow$ Community $\rightarrow$ Society $\rightarrow$ Supranational_.
