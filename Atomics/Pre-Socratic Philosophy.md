# Pre-Socratic Philosophy

organizations
constantly face the tension between 
change and continuity,
adaptation and stability, 
innovation and identity, 
flexibility and control.

Heraclitus = Philosopher of Flux, Becoming, Contradiction and Dynamic Adaptation
- River Philosophy
- Managers should see an organization as evolving system

Parmenides = Philosopher of being, Permanence, essence and stable identity 
- Managers should look beyond Surface-level Change to find something permanent 
- in Organization
- Core identity, Enduring Purpose, non-negotiable Values
