# welfare in Oligopoly

Welfare outcomes in an oligopoly depend heavily on whether firms compete or collude,

**Collusion vs. Non-Collusion:**

- Under **cartels/explicit collusion**, prices approach monopoly levels, maximizing deadweight loss and minimizing consumer welfare.
    
- Under **non-collusive models** (e.g., Cournot, Bertrand, or price wars), increased rivalry drives prices closer to marginal cost, increasing total social welfare.
