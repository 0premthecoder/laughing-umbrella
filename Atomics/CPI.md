# CPI

Measures Consumer's cost of living

Basis of Cost of Living adjustments (COLAs) in many contracts in DA (Dearness Allowance) and pension schemes

Cost is determined by
- Fixing the Basket (Some Objects is fix which consumer uses)
- Finding the price 
- Computing basket Cost
- Choosing a base year and computing index
	- CPI = $$100 \times {\text{cost of basket this year} \over \text{Cost of basket in base year}}$$
- Computing the inflation year
	- inflation rate = $$ {\text{CPI this year} - \text{CPI last year} \over \text{CPI last year} } \times 100$$

### Issues with CPI

Consumer might substitutes goods (called Substitution Bias)
Cpi overstate Inflation (as it doesn't calculate substitutes )
Improved Quality = Price increase
Cpi overstates increase in cost of living
