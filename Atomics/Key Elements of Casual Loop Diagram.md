# Key Elements of Casual Loop Diagram

#### 1. Variables

Condition states that can increase or decrease over a defined time horizon (e.g., _Service Quality, Employee Stress, Inventory Stock, Customer Retention_).

#### 2. Polarity & Influence Arrows

- **Same Direction / Positive Polarity ($+$ or $S$):** Indicates that the two connected variables move in the same direction, all else equal.
    
    - _Example:_ $\text{Service Quality} \xrightarrow{\quad+\quad} \text{Customer Retention}$ (Higher quality increases retention; lower quality decreases retention).
        
- **Opposite Direction / Negative Polarity ($-$ or $O$):** Indicates that the connected variables move in inverse directions.
    
    - _Example:_ $\text{Price} \xrightarrow{\quad-\quad} \text{Demand}$ (Higher price reduces demand; lower price increases demand).
        

#### 3. System Delays ($\vert{}\vert{}$)

Identifies time lags between cause and effect. Delays prevent instantaneous system responses and often cause managers to over-correct or misdiagnose issues.

- _Example:_ $\text{Training Investment} \xrightarrow{\quad+\quad \vert{}\vert{} \quad} \text{Workforce Productivity}$.
