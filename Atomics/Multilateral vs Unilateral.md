# Multilateral vs Unilateral

Unilateral Approach (Going It Alone)
- Unilateralism occurs when a single nation acts independently to achieve its goals, largely disregarding the input or support of other countries.  

Multilateral Approach (Working Together)
- Multilateralism involves multiple nations (two or more) working cooperatively through alliances or international organizations to address shared challenges.
