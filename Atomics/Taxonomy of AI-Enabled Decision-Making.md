# Taxonomy of AI-Enabled Decision-Making

- **Rule-Based AI:** Expert systems, decision trees, rule engines. Replicates human rules at scale; brittle to shifts/incomplete rules.
    
- **Statistical / Classical ML:** Regression, Random Forests, Gradient Boosting, SVMs. Predicts from historical patterns; high accuracy in stable environments, degrades under distribution shift.
    
- **Deep Learning & Neural Networks:** CNNs, RNNs, transformers. Vision, NLP, complex representations; powerful but opaque black-box.
    
- **Generative AI & LLMs:** GPT-4 class models, multi-modal systems, agents. Multi-step reasoning, planning, coding; productivity uplift, but risks hallucinations.
