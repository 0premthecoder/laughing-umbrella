# Characteristics of Perfect Competitive Market

1. Many buyer and sellers
2. The good offered for sale are largely the same
3. Firms can freely enter or exit the market

Because of 1 and 2 each buyer and seller is "price taker"

In the long-run equilibrium of a competitive market with free entry and exit and identical firms, price equals marginal cost, price equals minimum average total cost, and economic profit is zero
