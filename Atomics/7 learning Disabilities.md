# 7 learning Disabilities

- **"I Am My Position" (Role Identification):** Employees define themselves by their job titles rather than the broader organizational purpose, optimizing local functions at the expense of the overall system.
    
- **"The Enemy Is Out There":** A self-serving cognitive bias where teams blame external factors (regulators, competitors, market conditions) rather than auditing internal systemic structures.
    
- **The Illusion of Taking Charge:** Confusing aggressive, event-level firefighting with proactive, structural problem-solving.
    
- **Fixation on Events:** Focusing exclusively on immediate, short-term outcomes while remaining blind to long-term structural trends.
    
- **The Parable of the Boiled Frog:** The inability to perceive slow, gradual deterioration over time (e.g., Kodak's slow decline or Nokia's gradual loss of market dominance).
    
- **The Delusion of Learning from Experience:** Relying strictly on direct personal experience, ignoring the reality that major strategic decisions have consequences separated in time and space.
    
- **The Myth of the Management Team:** Teams maintaining an appearance of functional harmony while practicing "skilled incompetence"—avoiding honest vulnerability and collective inquiry to protect team dynamics.
