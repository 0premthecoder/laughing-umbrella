# Negative Externalities

A negative externality is ==an unintended cost imposed on a third party who is not involved in the original transaction==. 
Because these hidden costs aren't paid by the buyer or seller, the product's price remains artificially low, leading to overproduction and market failure.

e.g Chemical gets thrown in water polluting rivers which harms the Farmer's Production
