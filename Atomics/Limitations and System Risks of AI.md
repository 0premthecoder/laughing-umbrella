# Limitations and System Risks of AI

- **Distribution Shift / Concept Shift:** Pre-trained models fail during disruptions (e.g., COVID-19 invalidating pre-pandemic models).
    
- **Hallucinations:** Confident generation of factually false outputs.
    
- **Spurious Correlations:** Learns historical statistical associations rather than causal mechanisms.
    
- **Adversarial Brittleness:** Vulnerability to inputs engineered to exploit decision boundaries.
    
- **Opacity / Black-Box Nature:** Lack of explainability creating regulatory and accountability gaps.
    
- **Bias Amplification:** Replicating human/societal biases present in training data.
    
- **Lack of Common Sense / World Models:** Models are interpolators within training data, not robust generalizers.
