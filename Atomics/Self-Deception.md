# Self-Deception

- **Core Mechanism:** Convincing oneself that something is true simply because it is psychologically convenient, even in the face of contradictory evidence.
    
- **Self-Deception vs. Lying:** Lying is hiding the truth from _others_; self-deception is hiding the truth from _oneself_ to avoid painful actions (e.g., admitting strategic failure, redesigning products, or writing off bad investments).
    
- **Warning Signs:** Hearing phrases like _"Our customers will never leave us,"_ _"Disruption is just a fad,"_ or _"The problem is execution, not strategy."_
    
- **Remedies:** External benchmarking, customer loss analysis, pre-mortem analysis, red teaming, and decision journals to log original assumptions.
