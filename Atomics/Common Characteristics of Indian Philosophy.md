# Common Characteristics of Indian Philosophy

**Direct Experience** : Philosophy should be tested and verified in first person realization

**Practicality** : Use of Knowledge not just for the sake of it

**Acceptance** : Authority is accepted as a means of knowledge, [[Perception]] and [[Inference]]

**Open-Mindedness** : Schools engage in Opposing School's Arguments in details (Purva Paksha) before advancing their own

**Reasoning** : Ultimate goal is experimentation still Reliance on Reasoning and Logic

**Continuity** : Commentarial traditions run, in some schools, for over 2000 years without rupture of institutions

**Parallel-Growth** : Mutual respect and co-existence among Rival schools

**Shared acceptance on Knowledge** :  The law of moral causation (Karma) and Suffering (Dukkha) is seen in many theory though different remedy

**Holistic Orientation** : Self, Cosmos, and ethics are treated as facets of one integrated inquiry rather than separated disciplines
