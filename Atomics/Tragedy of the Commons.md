# Tragedy of the Commons

- **Mechanism:** Multiple actors draw on a shared, finite resource. Individual actors maximize short-term gains, while the cost of depletion is shared across all, eventually exhausting the resource.
    
- **Example:** Overfishing of cod stocks in Newfoundland, Canada, driven by larger vessels, efficient trawling, and high quotas, led to total population collapse.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Implement institutional solutions through privatization, external regulation, or community-governed self-management (Elinor Ostrom's framework).
