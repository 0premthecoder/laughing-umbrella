# Cognitive Biases

- **Source:** Heuristics and mental shortcuts used for speed.
    
- **Risk:** Leads to analytical errors and poor data interpretation.
    
- **Key Types:**
    
    - **Confirmation Bias:** Seeking, interpreting, or remembering only data that supports existing beliefs (e.g., focusing on 10 successful AI projects while ignoring 5 failures).
        
    - **Anchoring Bias:** Over-relying on the first piece of information received to calibrate subsequent estimates (e.g., sticking near an initial $500\text{Cr}$ project estimate despite updated $850\text{Cr}$ evaluations).
        
    - **Availability Bias:** Overestimating probabilities based on how easily recent or vivid examples come to mind (e.g., over-investing in cybersecurity immediately after a competitor suffers a breach).
        
    - **Overconfidence Bias:** Systematically overestimating knowledge, accuracy, or execution capability (e.g., startup founders projecting unrealistically high sales and low costs).
        
    - **Framing Effect:** Altering decisions based purely on presentation context (e.g., viewing a "90% job retention" plan favorably compared to a "10% job elimination" plan).
