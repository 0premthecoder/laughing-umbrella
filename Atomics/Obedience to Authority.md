# Obedience to Authority

- **Core Mechanism:** Uncritical acceptance of instructions, judgments, or assumptions from powerful figures without independent evaluation.
    
- **The Strategic Risk:** Authority substitutes for sound reasoning, leading team members to comply with flawed executive directives despite holding internal concerns.
    
- **Remedies:** Authority-neutral decision processes (e.g., asking leaders to share preferences last), requiring assumption sheets for proposals, and utilizing anonymous voting on key strategic bets.
