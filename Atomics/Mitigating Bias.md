# Mitigating Bias

Complete elimination of bias is impossible; mitigation requires structured processes and systematic friction.

#### Key De-Biasing Techniques

- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Assume failure upfront and work backward to identify risks before launching a project.
    
- **Red Teaming / Devil's Advocate:** Assign dedicated teams to challenge assumptions and attempt to break the proposed strategy.
    
- **Decision Records & Calibration:** Log predictions, confidence levels, and assumptions at decision time, then review actual outcomes to calibrate future judgment.
    
- **Psychological Safety:** Build an environment where junior employees can voice dissent without fear of career impact
