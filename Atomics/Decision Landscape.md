# Decision Landscape

- **High Frequency, Low Stakes (Operational):** Fully automated (e.g., inventory reorders, email routing).
    
- **High Frequency, Moderate Stakes (Functional):** AI-augmented; human reviews/approves (e.g., credit underwriting, demand forecasting).
    
- **Low Frequency, High Stakes (Strategic):** Human-led with AI scenario analysis/risk quantification (e.g., M&A, market entry).
    
- **Novel / Unprecedented Decisions:** Human-led with complexity judgment; historical data is absent/misleading (e.g., pandemic response).
    
- **Value-Laden Decisions:** Solely human moral agency; ethics, culture, restructuring (e.g., ESG, layoffs).
