# Firms Shuts down when

In short run => When Price is less than Average Variable Cost
- In the short run, fixed costs (like rent or specialized machinery) are [[Sunk Cost]]. 
- You must pay them even if you produce zero output Shutdown minimizes short-run losses to just the fixed costs

In Long Run => When Price is less than Average Total Cost
- In the long run, **all costs are variable**. There are no fixed or sunk costs. Contracts expire, and machinery can be sold
- **If Price < ATC**: The firm cannot cover its total cost per unit. It operates at a persistent net loss.
- **No barriers**: Since the firm can freely exit the industry without being trapped by fixed expenses, it will choose to leave permanently rather than lose money
