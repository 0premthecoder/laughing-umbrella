# Socratic Questioning

- **Origin:** Developed by Socrates in ancient Greece (470–399 BCE) as the _Elenctic Method_, created to prioritize the **discovery of objective truth** over simply winning arguments (which was the focus of the _Sophists_).
    
- **Core Philosophy:** Grounded in _epistemic humility_ (_"I know that I know nothing"_), relying on disciplined, systematic inquiry to expose contradictions, unearth hidden assumptions, and refine reasoning.
    
- **Modern Evolution:** Adapted into an educational and professional framework by **Richard Paul and Linda Elder** in the 1980s.
    
- **Business Context:** A structured inquiry process used by managers to evaluate the clarity, validity, and completeness of strategic claims before committing capital or operational resources.
