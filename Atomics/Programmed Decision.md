# Programmed Decision

Programmed decisions are 
- repetitive, routine, and governed by established decision rules.
- for standard recurring problems have been encountered before and solved, 
- the solution codifies in a procedure, policy, or algorithm.

The decision rule identifies the trigger (what situation calls for a decision) and specifies the
response (what the decision should be).

e.g 
Finance: Consumer loan decisions in a bank based on credit score thresholds.
HR: Hiring decisions following structured competency-based interview rubrics.

### Limitation

- Cognitive Resources might avoid genuine judgements
- Assumption of environment may become obsolete
