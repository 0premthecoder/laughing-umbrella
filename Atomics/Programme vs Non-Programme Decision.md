# Programme vs Non-Programme Decision

|**Programmed Decisions**|**Non-Programmed Decisions**|
|---|---|
|Repetitive and routine|Complex, important, and non-routine|
|Governed by established rules / algorithms|No established procedure; each case is unique|
|Speed and consistency are primary virtues|Creativity and judgment are primary virtues|
|Manageable through systems and procedures|Requires exploration, synthesis, and innovation|
|Risk: procedures become obsolete|Risk: cognitive bias and satisficing under pressure|
|Example: EOQ reorder point|Example: strategic pivot to new business model|
|Example: credit score cut-offs|Example: entering an entirely new industry|
