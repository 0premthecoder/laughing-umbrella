# Challenges to Performances

'Ten Afflictions' that compromise decision-making performance,

- **Structural Sclerosis:** Organizational barriers, silos, and rigid structures that obstruct workflow.

- **Decision Ambiguity:** Uncertainty regarding roles, responsibilities, or authority, leading to hesitation and confusion.

- **Process Analysis Paralysis:** Chronic delays, over-analyzing problems, and endless procedural loops that stall action.

- **Data Dysfunction:** Reliance on data that is missing, out-of-date, low-quality, or difficult to access.

- **Misaligned Measures:** Conflict between stated strategic goals and the actual incentives driving employee behavior.   

- **Blurred Vision:** Lack of strategic clarity, creating workplace confusion similar to the "fog of war."   

- **Consensus Overdose:** Gridlock caused by requiring unanimous agreement before moving forward.

- **Talent Deficiency:** Key roles staffed by individuals lacking the necessary skills, capabilities, or relevant experience.

- **Behavior Breakdown:** Inconsistency between leadership messaging and actual managerial conduct.

- **Performance Anemia:** Low motivation, insufficient drive, and disengagement across the workforce.
