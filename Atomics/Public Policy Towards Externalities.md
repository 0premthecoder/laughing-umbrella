### Why Economists Prefer Market-Based Instruments Over Direct Regulation

- **Cost Efficiency:** Different firms face varying pollution abatement costs. Pigouvian taxes allow low-abatement-cost firms to clean up more and high-cost firms to pay the tax, achieving target reductions at lower overall cost to society. Command-and-control imposes equal cuts across all firms regardless of cost efficiency.
    
- **Dynamic Incentives for Innovation:** Taxes and tradable permit systems continuously reward firms for cutting pollution beyond baseline targets, motivating them to adopt cleaner technologies. Regulation offers no incentive to reduce emissions beyond the legal limit.
    
- **Non-Distortionary Nature:** Unlike income taxes or production subsidies—which alter behavior away from market equilibrium—corrective taxes and subsidies push the economy toward the social optimum by forcing decision-makers to internalize their external costs or benefits.
