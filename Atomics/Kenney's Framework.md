# Kenney's Framework

|**Element**|**Description**|**Common Failure Mode**|
|---|---|---|
|**1. Problem Framing**|Define the right core problem, not symptoms.|Solving surface symptoms instead of root issues.|
|**2. Objectives**|Clarify goals and values to achieve.|Narrow focus on short-term metrics over broad goals.|
|**3. Alternatives**|Generate distinct, creative options.|Prematurely settling on obvious choices.|
|**4. Consequences**|Assess how options meet objectives.|Biased analysis favoring preferred options.|
|**5. Trade-offs**|Balance pros and cons across goals.|Evading hard trade-offs via vague logic.|
|**6. Uncertainty**|Identify and quantify key risks/unknowns.|Treating forecasts as certainties; overconfidence.|
|**7. Risk Tolerance**|Factor in willingness to take losses.|Assuming risk neutrality; ignoring downside risks.|
