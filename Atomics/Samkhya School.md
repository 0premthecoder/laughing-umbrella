# Samkhya School

Founder: Kapila, Smakhya Karika (Ishwarakrishna, 4th-5th CE)
Classification: Astika - Dualist, non-thestic

### Core Doctrine
- Reality Consists of two independent eternal principles
	- Prusha (Consciousness, plural & inactive )
	- Prakriti (matter/nature, active and involving)
- Prakriti evolves into 23 principles (tattvas)
	- intellect, ego, mind, the senses, and the elements - 
	- driven by Sattva(lucidity), Rajas (Activity), Tamas (inertia)

Methods: [[Perception]] & [[Inference]] with Testimony
- knowledge proceeds thorugh knowing difference bw both (Purush & Prakriti)

Western Counterpart: Mind-matter Dualism
