# Prisoner's Dilemma

==It shows why two completely rational people might not cooperate, even if it is in their best shared interest to do so==. 

The Classic Setup

- Two people are caught by the police and put in separate rooms. They cannot talk to each other.

- The police offer each person a deal to confess or betray the other.

- **If both stay silent (cooperate):** Each gets a short jail sentence of 1 year.

- **If one confesses and the other stays silent:** The confessor goes free, and the silent person gets a heavy sentence (3 to 4 years).

- **If both confess (betray each other):** Each gets a medium sentence (2 to 3 years).

The Core Problem

- **Individual Logic:** No matter what your partner does, confessing always gives you a better personal result than staying silent. (If they are quiet, you go free. If they confess, you get 2 years instead of 4.)
