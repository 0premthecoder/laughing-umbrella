# Various Measures of Cost

Total cost, TC = FC + VC
- Total cost of producing a given amount of output

Fixed costs, FC
- Do not vary with the quantity of output produced
- Incurred even if production is zero

Variable costs, VC
- Vary with the quantity of output produced
