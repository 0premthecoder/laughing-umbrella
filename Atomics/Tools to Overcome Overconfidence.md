# Tools to Overcome Overconfidence


| **Strategy / Tool**                   | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                    | **Example / Application**                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | **Key Benefit**                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Pre-Mortem Analysis**            | Proposed by psychologist Gary Klein.<br><br>  <br><br>Instead of asking: "Why will this project succeed?", ask: "Imagine this project failed completely. What caused the failure?" | **Project Management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Forces teams to identify potential failure points before starting.                           |

Scenario Planning**              | Popularized by Shell plc.<br><br>  <br><br>Instead of predicting one future, consider multiple futures.                                                                            | **AI Strategy:**<br><br>  <br><br>• Scenario A: Rapid adoption.<br><br>  <br><br>• Scenario B: Regulatory restrictions.<br><br>  <br><br>• Scenario C: Economic slowdown.                                                                  | Scenario planning reduces certainty illusions.                                               |

### Anti-Overconfidence Checklist

Before any major strategic decision, ask:

1. What evidence would prove us wrong?
    
2. Who disagrees with us?
    
3. What is the historical success rate of similar decisions?
    
4. What assumptions are most uncertain?
    
5. What could cause failure?
    
6. Are we confusing confidence with competence?
    
7. Have we sought independent views?
