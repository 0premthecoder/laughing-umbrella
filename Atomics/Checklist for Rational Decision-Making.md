# Checklist for Rational Decision-Making

- **Ego vs. Evidence:** Has evidence been prioritized over internal comfort or executive ego?
    
- **Inquiry vs. Certainty:** Have alternative explanations been systematically generated and tested?
    
- **Dissent vs. Consensus:** Was genuine dissent encouraged and evaluated before consensus was declared?
    
- **Learning vs. Defensiveness:** Is failure treated as diagnostic data or as something to hide?
    
- **Reality vs. Narrative:** Is the strategy based on verified market reality or a comforting internal story?
