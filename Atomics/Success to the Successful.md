# Success to the Successful

- **Mechanism:** Two or more activities compete for finite resources. Early success in one activity attracts disproportionate resources, starving alternatives regardless of their intrinsic merit.
    
- **Example:** Microsoft's core Windows and Office divisions received disproportionate resources, starving emerging opportunities like search, mobile OS, and cloud infrastructure.
    
- **Managerial Leverage:** Decouple resource allocation from short-term performance metrics by using independent resource pools or ring-fenced budgets.
