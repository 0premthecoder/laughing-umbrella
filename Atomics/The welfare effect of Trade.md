# The welfare effect of Trade

$P_D$ = Price of good domestically
$P_W$ = Price of good worldly

|                    | $P_D$ < $P_W$ | $P_D$ > $P_W$ |
| ------------------ | ------------- | ------------- |
| Direction of Trade | Exports       | Imports       |
| Consumer Surplus   | Lowers        | Rises         |
| Producer Surplus   | Rises         | Lowers        |
| Total Surplus      | Rises         | Rises         |
