# Nicomachean Ethics

Five Intellectual Virtue

|**Virtue (Term)**|**Meaning**|**Business Role**|
|---|---|---|
|**Scientific Knowledge** _(Episteme)_|Universal, logical facts and proofs|Data science, operations, financial models|
|**Technical Skill** _(Techne)_|Knowing how to build and execute|Product design, engineering, processes|
|**Practical Wisdom** _(Phronesis)_|Making good, ethical real-world decisions|Strategic judgment, leadership|
|**Philosophical Wisdom** _(Sophia)_|Deep understanding of fundamental truths|Long-term vision, core company values|
|**Intuitive Reason** _(Nous)_|Grasping core truths by instinct and experience|Gut feeling, expert pattern recognition|
