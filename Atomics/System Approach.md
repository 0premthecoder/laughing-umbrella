# System Approach

|**Feature**|**Hard Systems Approach**|**Soft Systems Approach**|
|---|---|---|
|**Problem Type**|Well-defined goals, clear objectives, measurable.|Open-ended, messy, contested, multiple interpretations.|
|**Core Assumption**|The system objectively exists and can be engineered/programmed.|The system is a subjective construct based on stakeholders' worldviews.|
|**Methodology**|Quantitative modeling, optimization, queuing theory, simulation.|Design thinking, perspective mapping, negotiated socio-technical action.|
|**Business Example**|Optimizing warehouse routing; Call center staffing models.|Redesigning urban mobility; Post-merger cultural integration.|
|**Primary Risk**|Ignoring internal politics and stakeholder meaning (too mechanistic).|Overlooking technical constraints (lacks empirical discipline).|
