# Jainism

A lineage of 24 tirthankaras, last = Mahavira (6th century bce)
Nastika - Pluralist realist

### Core Doctrine
Reality is genuinely many-sided (Anekatavada)
Jainism is built around the trirantha (three jewels - right faith, right knowledge, right conduct)
Strict commitment to ahimsa towards all living beings

Western Counterpart = Perspectivism
