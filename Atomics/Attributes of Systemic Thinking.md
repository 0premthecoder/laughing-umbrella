# Attributes of Systemic Thinking

- **Holistic Perspective:** Focuses on the whole system rather than isolated parts (e.g., viewing an organization as an interconnected network rather than independent functional silos).
    
- **Circular Causality:** Recognizes that variable $A$ affects $B$, but $B$ also feeds back to affect $A$ over time.
    
- **Dynamic Pattern Recognition:** Analyzes time-series trajectories, trends, and multi-quarter patterns rather than static snapshots.
    
- **Leverage Points:** Identifies strategic points in a system where targeted, minimal interventions yield significant, lasting improvements.
    
- **Fundamental vs. Symptomatic Solutions:** Addresses root causes rather than applying surface-level "band-aids" that perpetuate underlying issues.
    
- **Accounting for Delays:** Acknowledges the time lag between an action (cause) and its ultimate system-wide impact (effect).
    
- **Questioning Mental Models:** Surfaces, audits, and challenges unstated assumptions governing organizational behavior.
    
- **Embracing Complexity:** Operates effectively amidst ambiguity, non-linearity, and emergent system properties without reverting to premature oversimplification.
