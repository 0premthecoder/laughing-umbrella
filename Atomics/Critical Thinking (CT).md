# Critical Thinking (CT)

- **Role:** The "quality control department" of reasoning. Focuses on evaluation, analysis, and quality assurance of existing ideas and arguments.
    
- **Purpose:** Prevents bad conclusions, identifies hidden assumptions, and assesses evidence validity (does not primarily generate new options).
    
- **Cognitive Mode:** Operates predominantly through deliberate, analytical **System 2** processing.
    
- **Evolution:** Traced from Socratic Questioning $\rightarrow$ John Dewey (_How We Think_) $\rightarrow$ Watson & Glaser $\rightarrow$ Kahneman & Tversky.
