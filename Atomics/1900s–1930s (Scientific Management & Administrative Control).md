# 1900s–1930s (Scientific Management & Administrative Control)

- **F.W. Taylor:** "One best way" discovered via observation, measurement, and experimentation. Assumes problems have objectively discoverable right answers.
    
- **Henri Fayol:** Core administrative functions (plan, organize, command, coordinate, control) executed via rule-governed processes.
    
- **WWII & Post-War:** Growth of Operations Research (linear programming, queuing theory, Bayesian analysis) and quantitative management (e.g., Ford’s "Whiz Kids" like Robert McNamara)
