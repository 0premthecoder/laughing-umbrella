# Cost Benefit Analysis

A study Comparing the total cost and the total benefits 

For Public Good
- No market Price -> No clear Signal of value to Society
- Survey unreliable -> Beneficiary exaggerate gains, those harmed exaggerate losses
- Benefits are diffuse, intangible, and spread across millions of people
- MP Providing fund may not have technical expertise

For Private Good
- Having Market Price Automatically Reveals cost and benefit
