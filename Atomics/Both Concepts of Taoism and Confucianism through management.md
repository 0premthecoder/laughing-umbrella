# Both Concepts of Taoism and Confucianism


| **Concept**                    | **Core Meaning**                       | **Management Translation**                       |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Wu wei**                     | Action through non-forcing             | Decentralized, low-interference leadership       |
| **Yin–yang**                   | Dynamic balance of opposites           | Managing paradox and strategic rebalancing       |
| **Ren** _(benevolence)_        | Genuine care for others' welfare       | Sincere concern for employee/stakeholder welfare |
| **Li** _(ritual propriety)_    | Respectful conduct appropriate to role | Structured etiquette and organizational cohesion |
| **Junzi** _(exemplary person)_ | Leading through moral virtue           | Leadership legitimacy earned via character       |
| **Guanxi**                     | Reciprocal relational networks         | Trust- and relationship-based governance         |
