# 4 Stage of Creativity

**Graham Wallas’s 4 Stages of Creativity:**

1. **Preparation:** Problem framing and initial research.
    
2. **Incubation:** Unconscious cognitive processing (stepping away from the problem).
    
3. **Illumination:** The "Eureka!" moment when an unexpected connection surfaces.
    
4. **Verification:** Testing, refinement, and practical evaluation.
