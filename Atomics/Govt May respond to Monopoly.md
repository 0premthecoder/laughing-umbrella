# Govt May respond to Monopoly

|**Option**|**Policy Strategy**|**Key Mechanism / Objective**|
|---|---|---|
|**1. Increase Competition**|Boost market rivalry|Use antitrust/competition laws to block mergers and prevent price-fixing.|
|**2. Regulation**|Control firm behavior|Set price caps or mandatory pricing standards (e.g., $P = MC$ or $P = ATC$).|
|**3. Public Ownership**|Nationalize private firms|Convert private monopolies into state-run public enterprises.|
|**4. Inaction**|Do nothing|Leave the market alone if regulation costs exceed the efficiency losses.|
