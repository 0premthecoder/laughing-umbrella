# Toyota's 5 why

Asks "Why?" repeatedly until the root cause is reached - hidden beneath layers of symptomatic explanations

1. Why did the machine stop? Because the fuse blew.
2. Why did the fuse blow? Because of an overload.
3. Why was there an overload? Because the bearing was not lubricated.
4. Why was the bearing not lubricated? Because the lubrication pump was not circulating sufficiently.
5. Why was the pump not circulating? Because the pump shaft was worn.

Root cause: Replace the pump shaft.
Had the team stopped at "Why 1" (replace the fuse), the machine would have stopped again.

>Only Socratic persistence - refusing to accept the first plausible answer - yields genuine knowledge.
