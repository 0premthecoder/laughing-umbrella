# Role of RBI

Price Stability
>RBI aims to keep inflation under control
>Target: maintain inflation around 4% (with a band of 2%-6%)
>Too much money in economy -> prices rise -> inflation
>RBI controls money supply to stabilize prices

Support Economic Growth
>RBI ensures enough money flows in the economy to support production and employment
>Balances between controlling inflation and not slowing down growth
>Low interest rates -> more borrowing -> more investment -> growth
