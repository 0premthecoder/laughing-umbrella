# Cynefin Framework

pronounced = kuh-NEV-in

'the place of our multiple belongings'

was developed by Dave Snowden & co-
published with Mary Boone in Harvard Business Review, 2007.

The framework draws on complexity science, cognitive anthropology, Wittgensteinian
philosophy of language, and narrative-based knowledge management research.

The framework emerged from Snowden's dissatisfaction with existing decision-support
tools, which he observed tended to assume a relatively stable, knowable world.
