# Concept of Shintoism in Management

Shinto Doesn't have a single text it has evolved in Japan and have roots in [[Buddhism]] 

| **Concept**              | **Core Meaning**                                     | **Management Translation**                                       |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Kami**                 | Sacred essence inhabiting nature, objects, and tools | Reverence for craftsmanship (_monozukuri_) and workplace respect |
| **Wa** _(harmony)_       | Group cohesion and conflict avoidance                | Consensus-driven decisions and collective quality systems        |
| **Purity / Cleanliness** | Physical and ritual cleanliness as moral order       | 5S discipline and meticulous shop-floor housekeeping             |
| **Nemawashi**            | Behind-the-scenes groundwork for consensus           | Pre-meeting alignment so formal meetings ratify decisions        |
| **Ringi**                | Circulated proposal for sequential sign-off          | Bottom-up, distributed documentation and shared accountability   |
| **Ganbaru**              | Perseverance and disciplined effort as obligation    | Continuous improvement (_kaizen_) and self-directed commitment   |
