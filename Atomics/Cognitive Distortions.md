# Cognitive Distortions

- **Core Mechanism:** Systematic, irrational, exaggerated, or incomplete ways of interpreting reality and events.
    
- **Common Types in Business:**
    
    - **Catastrophizing:** Assuming the absolute worst outcome (_"If we cut prices, the brand will be destroyed"_).
        
    - **Overgeneralization:** Drawing sweeping conclusions from isolated data (_"One failed launch means we're bad at innovation"_).
        
    - **Black-and-White Thinking:** Viewing situations strictly as binaries (_"Either we fully automate or we become obsolete"_).
        
    - **Personalization:** Attributing complex system failures to a single person or group (_"Sales fell because marketing failed"_).
        
    - **Mental Filtering:** Focus exclusively on negative or positive data while ignoring the rest.
        
    - **Emotional Reasoning:** Placing feelings over objective data (_"I feel this acquisition is right, so it must be"_).
        
- **Remedies:** Structured diagnosis, separating facts from interpretations, generating at least three alternative explanations, and banning extreme language (_always, never, everyone, impossible_).
