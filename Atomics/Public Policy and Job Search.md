# Public Policy and Job Search

Govt employment agencies
provide information about job vacancies to speed up the matching of
workers with jobs.

Public training programs
aim to equip workers displaced from declining industries with the skills
needed in growing industries.

Unemployment Insurance
a govt program that partially protects workers' incomes when they
become unemployed
- Benefit: Gives more time to Search for a job may results in better job matches and thus higher productivity 

but it may lead to more frictional Unemployment 
- People have less incentive to search for a job
