# Five Design Principles

- _Calibrated Trust:_ Display model accuracy, confidence, and failure boundary conditions.
    
- _Preserving Human Agency & Accountability:_ Prevent "governance theater" (e.g., 5-second human-in-the-loop rubber-stamping); supply sufficient time, context, and override authority.
    
- _Algorithmic Transparency & Explainability:_ Employ techniques like LIME, SHAP, and attention visualization to uncover rationale.
    
- _Adversarial Robustness & Model Monitoring (MLOps):_ Implement governance for retraining, replacing, or falling back to manual processes upon drift.
    
- _Inclusive Design & Bias Governance:_ Utilize diverse development teams, disaggregated population evaluations, and mechanisms to address demographic/linguistic disparities.
